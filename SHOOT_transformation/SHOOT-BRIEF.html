The one shot that makes this film work

Folder 01_HOOK_WORST_DETAIL and folder 10_REVEAL_MATCH_HOOK must be the identical framing — same spot, same distance, same height, same angle, same lens. One dirty, one clean.

That match cut is the twist. Before you clean anything: tape the camera position on the floor and photograph your own screen so you can rebuild the frame exactly. If the two frames don’t match, the film has no turn and I cannot fake one.

Transitions — and what you must shoot to enable them

TransitionWhereWhat the camera must do
MATCH CUT
no effect		THE TURN, 01→10 Identical framing, dirty then clean. The only transition that matters. Its power is entirely in the frames being the same.
WHIP PANwork montage, 2–3× End a shot with a fast pan OFF and start the next with a fast pan ON, in the same direction. I blend the blurs. Opposite directions look broken.
WIPE-THROUGHinto the interior Let something cross the lens and black out the frame — a wheel, a closing door, your shoulder. Keep rolling through it.
DISSOLVE
0.12s		between work beats Nothing special — just the 4-second minimum so I have overlap.
DIP TO BLACK
0.16s		work → reveal Nothing to shoot. One breath so the reveal lands in clean air.

No whoosh on every cut. Five transitions in 45s, not fifteen. A transition on every cut announces the edit and hides the work.

Rules that decide whether the edit works

  • Never stop a move mid-shot. Start it, finish it, then stop recording.
  • 4 seconds minimum on every work shot.
  • The match pair (01 / 10) is non-negotiable. Tape the floor. Photo your screen.
  • Say the CTA whole, 3×, silence either side.
  • Shoot all the dirt first — folders 01, 02, 03 before you touch anything. No going back.
  • One shot per file. I cannot put one clip in two folders.
  • Don’t narrate the work. The only speech is the CTA.
  • Bloopers and false starts go in 00_TAKES_AND_BLOOPERS — last time a count-in got treated as content.
