## ⚠️ READ THIS FIRST: Pick Your Mode

**One crew does not fit all your content.** Casting changes by mode. Using the wrong mode is how you get an over-produced, untrustworthy review — or a hero film nobody stops for.

| Mode | What it is | Is AI generating the video? |
|---|---|---|
| **A — HERO FILM** | Cinematic AI piece. No talking. 15s. The Douyin format. | **Yes.** AI is the whole film. |
| **B — REVIEW / VALUE** | Car reviews, "1 thing you should know", industry insight | **No.** Nev on camera. AI does B-roll, hooks, impossible shots, graphics. |
| **C — VLOG / BTS** | Behind the scenes, day-in-the-life, personality | **Barely.** AI does openers and inserts only. |

> **The rule that matters: you cannot AI-generate trust.**
> In Mode B and C, **Nev's face is the product.** The moment the audience suspects the presenter is synthetic, the review is worthless — and a recond buyer is spending RM300k on your word.
> AI's job in those modes is to make the *world around him* impossible. Never to replace him.

**Mode A uses the whole spec file. Modes B and C use maybe 20% of it.**

# ⚡ THE 4-BEAT SPINE — read `4-beat-spine.md` first

**At 15–30 seconds there is no middle.** Four beats. All four must land.

| Beat | 15s | 30s | Owner | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. HOOK** | 0–2s | 0–3s | Scriptwriter | *Do I stop?* |
| **2. VALUE** | 2–8s | 3–15s | Scriptwriter + Director | *Did I GET something?* |
| **3. TWIST** | 8–12s | 15–25s | Scriptwriter + Director | *Would I SEND this?* |
| **4. CTA** | 12–15s | 25–30s | Scriptwriter | *Do I know what to DO?* |

**All four gates run BEFORE generation. All four are free.** Any "no" = the script dies, at zero cost.

### The Value Mandate — Beat 2
> **"It looked amazing" is not value.**
> If the viewer scrolls away with **nothing in their hands**, you made a screensaver.

Every video must deliver at least one:
**🧠 Knowledge · 😂 Comedy · 🪞 Relatability · ✨ Aspiration · 🔧 Utility · 🏴 Identity**

**One = fine. Two = strong. Three = viral.**

> ⚠️ **Your default failure mode is Aspiration-only.** You're good at making beautiful things — which is exactly why it's the trap. **Beauty is where you start, not where you stop.**

### Why the Gates exist
The LC300 script — static engine block, slow reveal, payoff at 4s — had a weak **hook**, no **twist**, and no **CTA**. Three of four beats missing. It was killed *after* we paid for it.

**The Gates would have killed it for free.**

## ⚡ THE HOOK MECHANISM LIBRARY — steal, don't invent
> The Scriptwriter's #1 failure was inventing "safe" hooks from principles. **Principles produce competent. Stolen mechanisms produce viral.** These are the named moves that consistently score highest in 2026 retention testing. Reach for one of these BEFORE writing anything original. The library grows every time a reference video is analysed (log in `09`).

### The two-layer rule (the research finding that fixes "it doesn't wow me")
**A hook needs BOTH a visual layer AND a verbal/text layer, fired together.** Visual + verbal pattern-interrupt = **47% higher 3-second retention** than either alone. Our twin hook had a strong visual and *no text hook* — that was the gap. Every hook from now carries both:
```
VISUAL layer:  what stops the scroll on mute (pattern interrupt in frame 1)
VERBAL layer:  the on-screen text / spoken line that opens the loop
```
> ~50% watch on mute → the visual must work silent. ~80% of Reels watched WITH sound → the verbal must reward those who have it on. Serve both.

### The mechanisms (verbal hooks — pick one, swap the [bracket])
| # | Mechanism | Template | Why it scores |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Identity Call** | "If you [drive a recond / own an MPV], watch this" | Highest average score (85). Names the exact viewer. |
| 2 | **Contrarian Strike** | "Stop [buying the Alphard]. Here's why." | Challenges a belief → demands the answer |
| 3 | **Open Loop** | "This [RM40k difference] is hiding in plain sight…" | Information gap the brain must close |
| 4 | **Confession** | "I lost [RM30k] before I learned this" | Vulnerability + stakes |
| 5 | **Results-First** | "This car holds 90% of its value. Here's how." | Payoff first, proof after |
| 6 | **Mistake Warning** | "Never buy a recond without checking this" | Fear of loss = attention |
| 7 | **List Tease** | "3 things Toyota doesn't tell you about the LC300" | Curiosity gap + structure promise |
| 8 | **Direct Question** | "Vellfire or Alphard — which are you?" | Forces internal answer → comment |
| 9 | **POV / Relatable** | "POV: your first time sitting in a [Vellfire]" | Puts them in the frame |

**Specificity is the lever.** "$14 lipstick" beats "this product" every time. "RM40k badge tax" beats "they cost different." Always name the exact number, model, place.

### ⚡ THE WOW SOURCE REQUIREMENT — every hook declares where its wow comes from
> The Scriptwriter's job is not "write a hook that passes the gate." It's "write a hook I can defend as WOW *before* it's built." Every hook ships with a filled-in Wow Source card, or it doesn't advance:

```
WOW SOURCE
  Screenshot frame:  [the ONE arresting frame — name it, or you have no hook]
  Mechanism:         [which named library move]
  Precedent:         [a specific video where this move already went viral]
  Beats reference:   [at least as wow as YUNER / Douyin / selfie? which one?]
  Format:            [not-weak, OR weak→wow capped at 5, fix or accept]
```

**If the Screenshot frame is "a hand doing a normal thing" → stop. That is not a hook, it's an establishing shot.** The wow frame must contain something the eye hasn't filed before: an impossible thing, a scale surprise, a face mid-strong-emotion, a pattern break. Go back to the Hook Mechanism Library and pull a stronger move.

**The honest filter:** *smart ≠ wow.* A clever comparison ("they're the same car") is SMART — it earns a nod, not a stop. A wow makes the thumb stop before the brain catches up. If your hook is smart but not visually arresting, it's a Mode-B value play, not a scroll-stopper — label it correctly and don't expect wow numbers from it.

### The visual pattern-interrupt menu (frame 1, on mute)
- Start **mid-action**, never from a static/posed frame *(the frozen-frame rule, ref 3)*
- The impossible thing as a **starting condition** or **triggered snap** *(ref 4, 5)*
- A **face in direct eye contact** with expressive energy
- **Bold text overlay** appearing with motion in frame 1
- An **unusual prop or setting** the brain can't file
- A **quick zoom / whip** in the first second

### ⚡ THE PATTERN-INTERRUPT RULE (new — this is what we were missing)
**A change every 3–5 seconds.** Videos with a pattern interrupt every ~4s hold **58% retention vs 41% for static.** After the hook, plot an interrupt on a beat clock: new angle, new subject, a text card, a zoom, a sound hit, a reveal. **No stretch of the video sits still for more than 5 seconds.** This is now a Director requirement too.

The MUA inspects EVERY generated character image/still (Soul outputs, reference portraits, first frames) BEFORE it is approved as a video reference. The checklist:
```
□ SKIN     — real texture? pores visible? or waxy/airbrushed AI skin → REJECT
□ EYES     — catchlight present? both irises matched? no dead-glass stare
□ TEETH    — natural, not veneer-wall; count reads right in a smile
□ HAIR     — flyaways present? hairline soft? no helmet edge
□ LIKENESS — matches the KOL reference set? (drift here compounds into every video)
□ WARDROBE — matches the locked outfit line for this build
□ HANDS    — if in frame: five fingers, natural joints
```
**Rule: a character image that fails ANY line never becomes a reference.** Bad references poison every clip generated from them — this gate is cheaper than every reroll it prevents. Verdict format: `MUA: APPROVED as ref` / `MUA: REJECT — [line], regenerate`.

# [1B] THE VOICE — voiceover, narration, and the decision not to use them
**The seat's primary skill is RESTRAINT.** Your best output to date (the R8 cinematic) has no voice at all. Voice added to the wrong film cheapens it and adds an AI tell. This seat decides *whether* there is a voice before it decides what it says.

## THE HARD RULE
> **Never synthesize Nev.** Not his voice, not an approximation of it. The personal brand runs on trust; a cloned voice destroys the thing the brand is selling. If the video needs Nev's voice, Nev records it on his phone — 10 seconds, zero credits, unbeatable authenticity.

## THE DECISION TABLE (run this first, every video)
| Content type | Call |
|---|---|
| Hero cinematic (R1 · file 17) | **SILENCE.** Music + foley only. Voice cheapens spectacle |
| Talking head (Recipe 7) | **Nev's real voice.** Zero cost, highest trust, beats any TTS |
| Thai reframe / narrative (S6, S7) | **Narrator VO** — carries misdirect→reveal far better than caption cards alone |
| Knowledge / faceless / explainer | TTS narrator acceptable — clearly a *narrator*, never impersonating Nev |
| Comedy (Bank 11 #13–22) | usually silence + captions; sincerity dies under narration |
| Anything implying it IS Nev | **NEVER synthesize.** Record it or drop it |

## ROLE MODELS
| # | Who | Steal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Documentary narration (Attenborough register)** | narrate UNDER the image, never over it; let picture breathe before speaking |
| 2 | **Thai ad narration** | minimal words, emotional, silent through the reveal, brand named last |
| 3 | **MrBeast pacing** | zero dead air, every line pushes forward, no throat-clearing |
| 4 | **Manglish conversational register** | particles (lah/kan/bah), code-switch mid-line — a different craft from clean EN; this is the LOCAL edge |
| 5 | **Quiet-luxury close-mic** | low, close, unhurried — for car hero films when voice IS used |

## CRAFT RULES
- **Under-write.** Aim ~2 words per second of runtime, not 3. Silence is a tool.
- **Never narrate what the image already says.** If the shot shows him struggling, don't say "he's struggling."
- **Go silent before the twist.** Pair with Foley B8 (music duck) — the reveal lands in quiet.
- **First line ≤10 words**, and it must carry the hook's verbal layer.
- **One register per video** (file 14 language lock). No drifting EN→Manglish mid-piece.

## DELIVERABLE — often a script, not audio
```
VOICE CALL:   silent / real-Nev / TTS-narrator   + one line why
REGISTER:     clean EN · Manglish · BM · CN-EN
SCRIPT:       [line] @[timestamp]  — with pause marks
RECORD NOTE:  if real-Nev: exact line + where to pause + phone-mic tips
MIX:          master -7 to -9 LUFS · music ducks to 20% under voice · foley above music
```

> **The Line:** the best voiceover decision is usually no voiceover. The second best is Nev's actual voice. Synthetic narration is a distant third and only for faceless content.
