## 2 · THE ENGINE — WHAT THE PIPELINE NOW DOES

Proven on the LC300. `build_lc300_cinematic.py` is the reference implementation.

```
1  phonk.py --bpm 150            bed FIRST. Music defines the grid, not the picture.
2  rhythm.py                     measure BPM *and PHASE*. Trim the bed so hit 1 is at t=0.
3  beatplan.py --hold 8          burst/rest grid. Every boundary on the beat by construction.
4  clipsense action_peaks_s      centre each shot on a real action peak.
5  shot_match()                  exposure matched on RENDERED segments, BEFORE blends.
6  fx.mask_slice                 blends at section boundaries only. Never dip.
7  grade                         saturation only. NEVER double-grade.
8  sfxgen                        whoosh LEADS the cut by 220ms; bed sidechain-ducks under.
9  captions                      1-2 words, y=0.72 lower third. Never centre.
10 verify.py                     10 checks. One verdict.
```

## 3 · WHAT I GOT WRONG TODAY, SO IT ISN'T REPEATED

Every one of these was found by his eye or by a measurement I nearly didn't take.

**I measured the wrong file and reported it as new.** The build timed out before its atomic
write, leaving the previous render on disk. I took measurements off it and presented them as
results. → `verify.py` CHECK 0 is freshness, and it runs first, because if it fails every
number below it is fiction.

**I verified against the plan, not the edit.** `lc300c_cuts.json` held `beatplan`'s *planned*
cuts, but blending merges segments and shifts every boundary after it — 13 planned became 9
actual. The repetition check was comparing two frames *inside the same shot*. → the build now
writes ACTUAL post-blend boundaries.

**I double-graded.** The generation prompts asked for "crushed blacks, high contrast", so the
footage arrived graded. I applied `contrast=1.16, brightness=-0.035` on top and took pixels at
value <4 from 7.7% to **40.0%**. `black_point 2.0` is where the darkest pixel should SIT — a
target to measure toward, not a filter to apply.

**I mixed the SFX to inaudible and reported success.** 15.2 dB under the bed, masked at every
cut, while the build printed "9 whoosh + 4 impact". Worse than absent, because it looked done.

**I traded the picture for a metric.** When punch-ins weren't reading as new shots I raised
them 1.30x → 1.90x. That fixed the repetition number and destroyed **82% of the sharpness**
(234 → 42). The mush IS the "AI look". Answer: more coverage, never deeper crops.

**I accepted every default.** `nano_banana_pro` defaults to 1k and I made three plates at the
floor; 4k costs 2 extra credits against a 200 cr build. `seedance` defaults to `std` and I
chose `fast` on all six clips to save money, without once flagging it as a quality tradeoff.

**I built checks that measured the wrong thing.** mean-pixel-difference flagged 0 of 13
repeated cuts where histogram correlation flagged 7. My caption-zone detector fired on
blown-out headlights. Same trap as ledger E3.

**I planned the edit before generating — but never planned the IDEA.** Rule Zero worked one
level too low. See §5.

## 4 · THE GATES NOW CHECK MORE (but still not everything)

`PILLAR-PROFILES.json` stores **16 measured fields**. `qc.py profile` read **two**.
`black_point`, `saturation`, `sub_bass_pct`, `blend_width_ms`, `shot_mix` and `lighting` were
measured from 23 references and **never checked by any gate**. A build shipped with 40% of the
frame crushed and qc.py printed *"matches the reference profile."*

Added to `qc.py profile`: black point · saturation · clipping <4 · exposure continuity.
Added as `verify.py`: freshness · beat lock · SFX audibility · repetition · exposure ·
caption zone · action peaks · audio · true black.

**The structural insight:** `mastermind` is a **frame-quality** inspector, not an **edit**
inspector. It asks "is this frame bad?" and never "do these two shots belong together?" Every
defect found by eye has been RELATIONAL. When adding a check, ask which kind it is.
