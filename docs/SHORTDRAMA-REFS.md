## WHY THESE FORMATS (the craft match)
Korean vertical micro-dramas run 60–90s/episode and live on 3-second retention —
the EXACT constraint of a Talyx short. Their craft: cold-open mid-conflict, one
emotional turn per episode, cliffhanger every exit, twist before the midpoint.
Japanese short film adds restraint: reveal-don't-explain, image-first storytelling.
Anime shorts add: maximum feeling per second with zero dialogue — pure Talyx.
