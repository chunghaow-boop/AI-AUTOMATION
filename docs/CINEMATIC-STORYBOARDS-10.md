## THE SPINE — identical for all 10, so only the content changes

Generated by `beatplan.py --bpm 150 --dur 16 --pillar car_cinematic`. **Every boundary is on
the beat by construction**, so `editsense.snap` has nothing to correct.

```
150 BPM · beat = 0.400s · 17 shots · 16.00s
median shot 0.80s  (target 0.77)   cuts/min 60.0   rate_variation 0.48
```

| # | in | len | kind | role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | 0.00–4.00 | 0.80 ×5 | **BURST A** | the ignition — 5 hard cuts, one every 2 beats |
| 5 | 4.00 | 2.00 | **HOLD** | the reveal. The car, whole, breathing |
| 6–10 | 6.00–10.00 | 0.80 ×5 | **BURST B** | the details — wheel, lamp, badge, stitch, screen |
| 11 | 10.00 | 2.00 | **HOLD** | Nev. The only shot with a human in it |
| 12–16 | 12.00–16.00 | 0.80 ×5 | **BURST C** | the payoff + loop back to frame 0 |

**Why bursts and holds, not a metronome.** The references cut in clusters one beat apart,
then rest: `8.27 / 8.63 / 9.03` and `14.80 / 15.20 / 15.60` — 0.40s apart at 145.8 BPM,
exactly one beat. A flat shot length measures `rate_variation ≈ 0` and reads as a strobe.
The LC300 review cut scored **0.01**. This spine scores 0.48.

**Blends: 2 of 16 seams = 12.5%** (target 20%, reference range 6–33%). Place them at the
two HOLD exits only — 4.00s and 12.00s — as section punctuation, `d=0.40s` (inside the
measured 240–560ms band). Everything else is a **hard cut at 33–67ms**. Use `mask_slice` or
`mask_wipe`; **never `dip`** — it fades through black and will trip the blank-frame gate
legitimately.

**Grade: crushed.** black point **2.0**, saturation **91.5**. This is the inverse of the
review grade — do not reuse the LC300 review settings.

**Light: night, hard artificial, the car emits its own light.** Headlights, taillights,
glowing brakes, cluster, illuminated grille. Not dusk. Not a studio void. This came out of
the reference study and it is the single strongest visual signature of the pillar.

**Shot mix target:** 30% exterior · 15% wheel/brake · 15% light macro · 25% interior ·
10% badge · 5% human.

**Sound:** drift phonk 140–170 BPM. TR-808 cowbell is not optional. 808 distorted and
pitch-sliding, not clean sub. **Sub-bass 60–92% of the signal** — measure it, don't trust
the bed's name. `phonk.py` at 150.

**Captions:** lyric-synced, **1–2 words**, huge, centre-frame, on the beat. Not sentences.

## THE 4 GENERATIONS PER CAR

17 shots come from 4 clips by reframing — the `build_s450` pattern. **Reframe in space, not
time**: Seedance clips are front-loaded and the tail drifts.

| gen | content | feeds shots |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | exterior front 3/4, night, lights igniting | 0,1,2 · 5 (hold) · 16 (loop) |
| **B** | the signature detail — per car, see below | 3,4 · 6,7 |
| **C** | interior, ambient lit, screens glowing | 8,9,10 |
| **D** | **Nev with the car** | 11 (hold) · 12,13 |
| A/B/C reframes | punch-ins | 14,15 |

**R6 conflict — read this.** Never place a punch-in adjacent to its own source; that is a
jump cut, and ledger D3 already records *"coverage crops produced repetition, not variety."*
The table above interleaves deliberately: shot 16 returns to gen A only after 5 shots away.

## IDENTITY — the rule that cost 87 cr last time

**Two named subjects per video: Nev and the car.** Neither is ever generated from text alone.

```
1  NEV PLATE      ONE TIME, reused across all 10.        ~2 cr
2  CAR PLATE      per car, per model, before any video.  ~2 cr each
3  LOOK AT BOTH   confirm the signature feature is present
4  seedance_2_0 image_references [nev_plate, car_plate]  — it takes BOTH
```

`seedance_2_0` accepts **plural** `image_references`, so gen D carries Nev *and* the car on
one generation. The single-`start_image` pattern in CLAUDE.md cannot do this and should be
updated.

A text-only prompt for "2026 Toyota Crown" returned a generic crossover and shipped. Two
credits would have caught it.

# BEFORE ANY OF THESE RENDER

```
phonk.py --bpm 150                      bed FIRST. The grid comes from the music.
rhythm.py BED.wav                       confirm BPM + grid
beatplan.py --bpm 150 --dur 16 --json   the spine above
clipsense.py --scan work/               best_in_s, action peaks, shot size
editsense.py                            snap + score. R1-R6.
reverse.py refs/car_cinematic --mine CUT.mp4 --mine-cuts plan.json
verdict.py + qc.py profile --pillar car_cinematic
```

`--mine-cuts` matters: the detector found **2 of 7** cuts in the LC300 file and reported
`rate_variation 1.0` where the truth was **0.01**. Without declared cuts the diff flatters
the edit.

**The standing gap: zero posts.** Every target here is engineered-for, not measured.
