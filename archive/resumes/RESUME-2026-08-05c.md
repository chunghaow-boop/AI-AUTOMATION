## THE BLOCKER THAT STOPS EVERYTHING

**There is no bed and there cannot be one from the library.** All 25 tracks in `assets/bgm`
and `assets/pillars/car_cinematic/bgm` are 140–165 BPM drift phonk. This build needs
**100.00 BPM measured, warm, half-time, no cowbell, no distorted 808.**

Reaching into the phonk library here would repeat the ORIGINAL Crown failure in the opposite
direction — ledger `car cinematic` L6 records a Crown build with a 90 BPM marimba bed under
a car edit, judged wrong on tempo, timbre, distortion and signature instrument.

Generate via `tools/bgmgen.py`, then `rhythm.py` for BPM **and grid OFFSET**, and trim so hit
1 lands at t=0. A generated bed once put its first transient at 163ms; trimming took
cut-to-music from ~34ms to a median 2.5ms. A *constant* deviation is a phase error, not
sloppiness.
