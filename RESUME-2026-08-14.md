### L170 + the new mechanical check
`tools/clipgate.py` now detects **internal cuts** and ships with `--selftest` (synthesises
a one-take clip and a two-shot clip, asserts no-false-positive AND no-miss). It WARNs
rather than regenerates, because D proved an internal cut can be an improvement — what it
forces is that each side gets judged separately.

> **The calibration trap, and it is the real lesson.** The first version differenced frames
> at the existing `step=3` subsample and **missed D entirely** — 0.125s-apart frames make
> ordinary motion look like a cut, inflating the mean until a real cut fell to 3.0x, under
> the 3.5x threshold. Full-rate differencing put the same cut at 6.1x. The subsample made
> the check vacuous for precisely the high-motion clips most likely to contain a cut.
> **The selftest alone would NOT have caught this** — the synthetic splice is so violent it
> survives any subsample. It took replaying the check against the real batch and diffing it
> against the full-rate truth table.
> **A negative control proves a check FIRES. Only real footage proves it is CALIBRATED.**
