## PART 1 — THE AUDIENCE AVATARS (write at ONE, never at "viewers")

Every video names its primary avatar before the script. A hook aimed at everyone stops no one.

| # | Avatar | Who | Their pain | What stops their thumb | Buys |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The First-Car Kid** | 20–26, first salary, TikTok-native | "Can I even afford anything?" scared of being cheated | price-ladder content, "RM___ gets you THIS", mistake warnings | Myvi-budget recond, dreams bigger |
| 2 | **The Family Upgrader** | 30–45, 1–3 kids, FB Groups user | space vs budget vs safety; spouse must agree | MPV comparisons, hidden-cost truths, "the family verdict" | Vellfire/Alphard/Serena recond |
| 3 | **The Resale-Anxious Uncle** | 40–60, done this before, trusts nobody | "recond = problem car" fear; mileage tampering | mechanic confessions, inspection checklists, depreciation races | holds cash, buys when convinced |
| 4 | **The JDM Dreamer** | 18–35, car-culture identity | wants the badge/stance, budget says otherwise | hero films, night/neon looks, spec deep-dives, the one feature | aspires; shares & comments hardest |
| 5 | **The Silent Businessman** | 35–55, buys for image, DMs never comments | time-poor; wants status without research | quiet-luxury aesthetics, badge-tax logic, "arrived" energy | highest ticket, converts via DM |

**Rules:**
- The Strategist names the avatar in every brief. The Scriptwriter writes the hook in that avatar's inner voice.
- Avatar → platform: 1 & 4 live on TikTok · 2 & 3 live on Facebook/Groups · 5 watches everywhere, silently.
- The CTA must match the avatar (4 comments for war; 5 needs a DM keyword; 3 needs the checklist save).

## PART 2 — THE VOICE & LANGUAGE GUIDE (the local resonance lever)

The single cheapest differentiation vs generic AI content: **sounding like us.** Language is chosen per video, deliberately.

| Mode | When | Example caption |
|---|---|---|
| **Clean English** | Mode A hero films, Avatar 5, brand work | "One badge. RM40,000." |
| **Manglish** ⭐ default | Most content, Avatars 1–4 | "the offer that hurts 😮‍💨 kena tawar sampai sakit hati" |
| **BM lead** | FB Groups reach, Avatar 2/3 heartland | "Benda ni diorang tak tunjuk kat showroom" |
| **CN/EN mix** | Chinese-market units (Alphard/Vellfire culture) | "这台 Vellfire… same car, 差 RM40k 😅" |

**Manglish particles are precision tools, not decoration:** *lah* (soften/assert) · *kan?* (invite agreement → comments) · *bah* (Sabah marker — USE IT, it's home-ground identity) · *jangan main-main* (warning energy) · *confirm* (verdict).

**Rules:**
- ONE language mode per video. Code-switch inside lines, not between beats.
- Hooks hit harder in the viewer's inner voice: Avatar 2 thinks in BM/Manglish, not BBC English.
- Sabah identity ("bah", KK landmarks, local plate references) = J4 Local's cheat code. Kuala Lumpur pages can't fake it.
- Numbers always in RM with commas: "RM40,000" reads; "40k USD-style" doesn't.

## PART 3 — THE SERIES PILLARS (repeatable formats that compound)

One-offs restart from zero. A series teaches the algorithm AND the audience what to expect. Every video belongs to a pillar.

| # | Pillar (series name) | Format | Recipe | Avatar | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Recond Truth #N** | one insider fact they don't show you | R2 4-beat | 2, 3 | weekly anchor |
| 2 | **RM___ Gets You** | price-ladder reveal, one budget per ep | R2/R4 | 1 | weekly |
| 3 | **The Face-Off** | A vs B verdict (Vellfire/Alphard energy) | R3 chaptered | 2, 4 | biweekly |
| 4 | **Night Shift** | cinematic hero film, one car, night/neon | R1 hero | 4, 5 | biweekly flex |
| 5 | **Check Before You Buy** | one inspection point per ep, save-bait | R2 | 3 | weekly |
| 6 | **The Import Diary** | auction→ship→port→driveway journey | R3/R5 | all | when units land |

**Rules:**
- Series title card is a fixed visual asset (same font/position every episode) — recognition in frame 1.
- Numbering ("#12") signals depth → binge-scrolling the profile → session watch time → algorithm reward.
- Each pillar has a locked CTA ("Save this checklist" for #5, "Team A or B?" for #3).
- The Strategist's brief states: pillar + episode number + avatar + language mode. No orphan videos.

## The Line

> **"Viewers" is nobody. A persona is someone. A series is a promise.**
> Aim every video at one person, in their language, inside a format they already trust —
> that's how content stops being output and starts being a channel.
