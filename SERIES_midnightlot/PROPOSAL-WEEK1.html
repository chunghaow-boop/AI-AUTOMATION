TALYX ORIGINALS · AI SHORT DRAMA · PROPOSAL

MIDNIGHT LOT · 午夜车场

"Every recond car had a first life. At midnight, they tell it."

REFERENCE KEYFRAME (AI, generated for this pitch — becomes the production style ref) · series poster frame — Nev meets Haru

A 7-episode, one-week vertical drama, fully AI-generated. The lot is ours. The cars are our stock. The hero of every episode is a car we can actually sell.

Format
7 daily episodes · 55–75s each · vertical 9:16 · 720p (cost strategy)
Cast
NEV (our existing AI persona, identity refs locked) + HARU, a 2018 Toyota Harrier fresh off the ship — voiced, never lip-synced (the car "speaks" through light pulses in the DRLs)
Why a Harrier
THE iconic recond model in Malaysia — instantly recognisable to our exact buyer
Proven model
Serialized like Douyin's 《末法道士》 AI franchise (S3, contest flagship) · emotional engine of the viral "if your 10-year car could speak" W212 film — the two references analysed for this pitch
Compliance
AI-generated label on every episode (platform-mandatory, already our policy)

Why 1 week = 1 topic (the boss logic)

We have four series concepts. Instead of betting a quarter on one, we run a 4-week pilot month — one concept per week, seven episodes each. Same persona, same lot, same posting slots. At the end of the month the retention numbers pick the series; we double down on the winner and keep the runners-up as recurring segments.

Week arc — hook, twist, CTA of the WHOLE week

WEEK HOOK
A dealer locks up his lot at midnight — and one of the cars says his name. (Ep 1 cold open, no context, 2s.)
WEEK TWIST
Ep 5: the "flaw" Nev finds on Haru is the proof of her best day, not her worst — the moment the audience's fear of recond cars ("what is it hiding?") is inverted into the reason to trust one that's honestly documented.
WEEK CTA
Ep 7, spoken by Nev over a real handover: "Every car on this lot has a story like hers. Come meet them — or PM us, and we'll introduce you."
Meaning
A recond car is not a second-hand object; it's a first life looking for a second one. The series converts the market's biggest fear (unknown history) into its warmest promise (documented history).

The house prompt blocks (appended to EVERY video prompt below)

+ STYLE

Vertical 9:16. REAL PHOTOGRAPHY, NOT A RENDER: true skin and paint texture, natural handheld micro-shake, shallow depth of field, practical light only. Continuous action first frame to last, no internal cuts. generate_audio: true (diegetic sound only — no music, no voice; VO is added in the mix).

+ NEGATIVE

CGI, videogame look, plastic or waxy surfaces, stock-photo gloss, extra fingers, warped hands, floating objects, NO BRAND MARKS beyond the car itself, no legible number plates, no legible text on signs or screens, no crowds.

+ IDENTITY REFS

Every shot of HARU carries HARU_PLATE as start_image / image_reference. Every shot of NEV carries the locked persona refs (facecheck on every seam). Memory-timeline shots carry JP_HOUSE / BOY / FATHER plates. One character-set per shot — never two new identities in one generation.
