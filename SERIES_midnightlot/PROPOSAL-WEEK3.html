Week arc — hook, twist, CTA of the WHOLE week

WEEK HOOK
A rival lot opens across the street at RM4k under our prices — and our best customer walks out politely. (E1–E3.)
WEEK TWIST
E5: the customer returns soaked at the night gate, betrayed by the cheap car — and Nev opens the gate with zero gloating and fixes it free. Grace as strategy.
WEEK CTA
E7, whole: 'Cheap is the most expensive thing in this market. If someone sold you a car with a story it never told you — PM us. We'll read its diary with you. Free.'
MEANING
Price wars are won at the auction, not the signboard — and reputation is what you do when your rival's customer knocks on YOUR gate in the rain.

The house prompt blocks (appended to EVERY video prompt below)

+ STYLE

Vertical 9:16. REAL PHOTOGRAPHY, NOT A RENDER: true skin and paint texture, natural handheld micro-shake, shallow depth of field, practical light only. Continuous action first frame to last, no internal cuts. generate_audio: true (diegetic only — no music, no voice; VO is added in the mix).

+ NEGATIVE

CGI, videogame look, plastic or waxy surfaces, stock-photo gloss, extra fingers, warped hands, floating objects, no brand marks beyond the car itself, no legible plates, no legible text, no crowds.

+ IDENTITY REFS

NEV = locked persona refs, facecheck on every seam. Each patient car gets ONE plate generated on arrival and carried as start_image in every shot of it. One character-set per shot.
