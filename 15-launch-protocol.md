## PART 1 — TIMING (Malaysia time, audience-local)

| Platform | Window | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook Reels** (your #1) | **6–9pm weekdays** · 9pm = highest engagement slot of the day | evening "couch mode" |
| Facebook feed/Groups | 9am–12pm weekdays | Wed/Thu strongest days |
| **TikTok** | 6–11pm evenings · **Saturday is TikTok's best day** (worst for FB) | 11am–1pm secondary |
| IG Reels | 6–9pm | mirror FB |

**Rules:** never post into a dead hour (3pm trough, past midnight) · one platform's peak at a time — FB-first at 7–8pm, TikTok next evening or Sat, IG after · re-check your own insights every ~90 days; audience patterns drift.

## PART 2 — PRE-LAUNCH CHECKLIST (before upload)

```
□ POST TEXT: hook line ≠ caption — the caption ADDS (context, the question, or the number)
□ Language mode matches the video (file 14) — a BM video with an English caption breaks it
□ Hashtags: 3–5 max, specific (#recondmalaysia #vellfire #sabah) — never 20 generic
□ DISCLOSE AI (label/tag) — TikTok penalty rule
□ PINNED COMMENT drafted BEFORE posting: the planted argument or the answer-tease
□ First-hour REPLY SHIFT scheduled — do not post then disappear (posting at 7pm = you're free 7–8pm)
□ Group seeding list ready: 2–3 relevant FB Groups, shared with a native line, not a dump
□ Story/status announce ready (drives the first-minute viewers that seed the pool)
```

---

## PART 3 — THE FIRST-HOUR DRILL (the 80% window)

```
0–5 min    Story-share it · send to 3–5 real people who WILL engage (the seed)
0–15 min   Post the PINNED COMMENT (the war-starter: "Team Vellfire or Team Alphard?")
0–60 min   REPLY TO EVERY COMMENT — with a follow-up QUESTION, never "thanks 🙏"
           ("you'd take the Alphard? even at +RM40k?" → second comment from same user
            = the friend-to-friend signal FB weights hardest)
15–30 min  Share to the 2–3 Groups with a native intro line
45–60 min  Pin the best organic comment if it beats yours
```

**The reply rule:** a reply that asks a question doubles the thread. Comments-between-people is Facebook's strongest distribution signal — you are one of the people.

---

## PART 4 — THE 24-HOUR READ (closing the loop)

```
□ Pull: 3-sec rate · avg % viewed · shares · comments · traffic sources
□ OVERLAY predicted vs actual retention curve (the judges' per-beat prediction)
   — the gap per beat = the calibration signal → log it in 09
□ Verdict: which beat under/over-performed the prediction, and WHY (one line)
□ Route: strong → next episode of the pillar this week · weak → the failing beat's
   seat gets the lesson · dead in pool 1 → hook autopsy by J0
□ Winners only: consider boosting AFTER organic proves it (never boost a loser)
```
