## THE CATCH THAT PAID FOR THE WHOLE GATE

**Not one of the nine source prompts contains a single word of audio direction — and
source F actively forbids the one sound the video is about.**

F's prompt says the car is *"ALREADY under load as the clip opens."* A state change that
has already happened at frame zero cannot be heard happening. There is no crank, no catch,
no rise anywhere in nine prompts. `FOLEY[12] = 0.0dB` would have been applied to a clip
containing, at best, steady engine noise — and `FOLEY` is a mix-gain table, it cannot remove
combustion the model generated into the other eight clips.

The entire differentiator lived in a gain table applied to audio nobody had asked for.
202.5cr, unrecoverable, and `clipqc` would only have caught it at ingest on four of the
nine sources.
