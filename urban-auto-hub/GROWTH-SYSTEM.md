2. **Number before adjective.** "RM168,000" lands. "Sangat berbaloi" does not.

# PART 5 · CONTENT SYSTEM

## 5.1 · THE 3-SECOND RULEBOOK — the highest-leverage page in this file

Your north star is the 3-sec rate. Every one of these is a mechanical rule, checkable
before upload.

```
□ FRAME 1 CONTAINS THE PAYOFF, NOT THE SETUP
  Not "hi guys, today we look at…" — start mid-action. If the video is about a
  RM40,000 price gap, RM40,000 is on screen at 0.0s.

□ NO LOGO, NO INTRO, NO TITLE CARD IN THE FIRST 3 SECONDS
  Every frame spent on branding is a frame spent losing 86% of your audience.

□ THE FIRST WORD IS SPOKEN BEFORE 0.5s
  Silence at the top reads as a dead video and gets scrolled.

□ BURNED-IN TEXT IS UP BY 0.3s AND IS 4 WORDS OR FEWER
  It must be readable at a glance, muted, one-handed, on a phone.
  Most Facebook viewing is sound-off. The hook must work with no audio at all.

□ SOMETHING MOVES IN FRAME 1
  A static open is a scroll. A hand entering frame, a door closing, a push-in.

□ THE HOOK NAMES A NUMBER, A PLACE, OR A PROBLEM
  Numbers: "RM168,000." Place: "Kereta ni kat KK." Problem: "Meter ni tipu ke tak?"
  Abstract hooks ("let's talk about value") die.

□ NO TWO CONSECUTIVE DAYS USE THE SAME HOOK STYLE
  (already in WEEKLY-TASK — it is also a 3-sec rule; repetition trains the scroll)
```

### The hook A/B rule — the only way this number actually moves

**Shoot two openings for every video.** Same body, two different first-3-seconds. Post
version A. If the 3-sec rate is below 20% at 24 hours, **delete and repost with version
B at the next slot.** Log both in the tracker.

This costs about 90 seconds on the shoot day and is the single fastest way to learn what
your specific Sabah audience stops for. Without it you are guessing for another 90 days.

> A deleted-and-reposted Reel is not penalised in any way that matters at your volume. A
> 14% Reel left up is a dead asset teaching the algorithm that your page is unremarkable.

# PART 7 · ENGAGEMENT — THE BIGGEST GAP IN THE CURRENT SYSTEM

Facebook's ranking rewards **Meaningful Social Interactions** — long comments, shares to
Stories, real back-and-forth. Reels ranking specifically weights **shares/sends, saves,
completions, profile visits and follows**. Every one of those is a *person doing something*,
and people do things when a person, not a page, is talking to them.

At 584 followers your own comment section cannot generate that volume. **You have to go and
get it.**

## 7.1 · The daily engagement block — 90 minutes, non-negotiable

Full-time means this happens every working day. Put it in the calendar as an appointment.

```
30 min  INBOUND    Every comment and DM on your posts answered.
                   Reply with a QUESTION, never "thanks 🙏".
                   A reply that asks a question doubles the thread, and a second
                   comment from the same user is the friend-to-friend signal
                   Facebook weights hardest.

30 min  OUTBOUND — GROUPS
                   10 substantive comments in Sabah/Malaysia car groups.
                   Substantive = answers the question, names a specific thing,
                   no link, no pitch. §7.3.

30 min  OUTBOUND — CREATORS & DEALERS
                   5 comments on other Malaysian car creators' posts
                   (the PLAYBOOK list: Aunty Nora, CARZON, G-Mart, Jasrul,
                    Leong Sabah, Cartell KK, Boss Bryan)
                   + 5 on KK dealer pages.
                   Early, substantive, funny or useful. Never "nice car 🔥".
```

**Target: 75 outbound comments per week.** This is the growth engine at your size. It is
also unglamorous, which is why almost nobody does it, which is why it works.

## 7.2 · What a good outbound comment looks like

| ✗ Dead | ✓ Alive |
|---|---|
| "Nice car 🔥🔥" | "Grade 4.5 kan? Interior tu nampak macam baru ja. Auction sheet ada tunjuk repair mana-mana?" |
| "Great content bro" | "Betul ni. Kat Sabah lagi teruk — jalan Penampang tu memang makan suspension. Ground clearance kena kira dulu." |
| "Check out our page" | *(never)* |
| "How much?" | "Kalau harga macam ni, monthly dalam RM1,800 kan? Tempoh berapa tahun tu?" |

**The rule: add a fact or ask a real question.** The goal is the *reader of the comment*
tapping your name — not the poster liking you. You are farming profile visits.

```
   button, because they are already inside Facebook.
   Spoken CTA: "Comment 'NAK' kalau nak details" → you DM them the wa.me link.
   The comment is free engagement AND a warm lead. Two jobs, one CTA.

3  KEEP THE NUMBER SPOKEN AND ON SCREEN for the people who prefer to save it.
   But it is no longer the primary path.
```

## 7.5 · Fix the comment rate — lower the cost of answering

4 comments in 28 days is not a reach problem — even at the low end of the derived view
range (§0), that is under 0.2%. It is an **answer-cost** problem. "Which would you pick and why?" demands an opinion and a sentence. Most people
won't.

**Ask questions answerable in one word or one letter:**

| Answer cost | Example | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| High ✗ | "Apa pendapat korang pasal unit ni?" | ~0 |
| Low ✓ | "A atau B? Comment satu huruf ja." | many |
| Low ✓ | "Vellfire ke Alphard? Satu perkataan." | many |
| Low ✓ | "Berapa korang rasa harga ni? Teka ja." | many — guessing is fun and costs nothing |
| Low ✓ | "Comment 'NAK' kalau nak checklist ni." | many + a warm lead |

**The pinned comment is a planted first answer.** An empty comment section is socially
expensive to enter; a section with one comment in it is not. Post yours within 60 seconds,
in Nev's voice, taking a side: *"Aku team Alphard. Interior menang."*

# PART 8 · GROWTH

## 8.1 · The four loops

```
LOOP 1 · THE HOOK LOOP           (drives: reach)
  shoot 2 hooks → post A → read 3-sec rate at 24h → <20% repost with B
  → log which hook style won → PLAYBOOK.md gains a pattern
  → next week's hooks are picked from proven, not guessed
  ⟳ compounds weekly. This is the loop that moves the north star.

LOOP 2 · THE COMMENT LOOP        (drives: content quality + trust)
  outbound comments → profile visits → follows
  → real questions arrive in comments/DMs → bank/questions/
  → each question becomes a video → that video attracts the same buyer
  ⟳ your audience writes your content brief

LOOP 3 · THE DEALER LOOP         (drives: revenue + stock supply)
  good video for dealer A → dealer A gets enquiries → you send him the numbers
  → he gives you better access (documents, lot, time) AND tells dealer B
  → dealer B joins → more stock, more variety, more content
  ⟳ this is the actual business. Feed it deliberately.

LOOP 4 · THE PROOF LOOP          (drives: B2B credibility)
  Sunday social-proof post → screenshots of real convos and handovers
  → buyers see other buyers → dealers see the page works
  ⟳ the cheapest trust asset you have, and it costs one post a week
```

## 8.2 · The dealer flywheel — run it explicitly

**Monthly, per dealer partner**, send a one-page report. Not a chat message. A document.

```
URBAN AUTO HUB × [DEALER] — [Month]
  Videos featuring your stock:     N
  Total views:                     N
  3-sec rate:                      N%
  Comments:                        N
  Enquiries routed to you:         N        ← the number he cares about
  Units that found an owner:       N
  Best-performing unit:            [model] — N views
  Next month we'd like to shoot:   [ask]
```

This does four things at once: proves value, justifies a retainer, gets you better access,
and makes you impossible to replace with "my nephew who has a phone."

**Target: 2 active partners by day 30, 4 by day 90.** Approach new dealers with a *sample* —
shoot one of their units free, show the numbers, then quote.

## 8.3 · Collaboration — the fastest legitimate follower source

At 584 followers, borrowing an audience beats building one.

- **Sabah-local creators** (Leong Sabah, Cartell KK, Boss Bryan): propose a **collab post**
  — Facebook collab posts appear in both audiences' feeds natively. Offer the shoot, the
  edit and the footage for free. You want the distribution, not the fee.
- **Dealer pages:** every dealer partner should reshare their own video to their page. Put
  it in the agreement. It costs them nothing and it is free reach.
- **Adjacent, non-competing local businesses:** insurance agents, tyre shops, detailers,
  workshops. Same buyer, no overlap, easy trade.

## 8.4 · What NOT to do

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| Buy followers or engagement | Kills your reach permanently by poisoning the audience signal Facebook uses to find real viewers |
| Boost a post that hasn't proven organic | You are paying to distribute a video the algorithm already declined. Boost winners only, after 48h. |
| Post more than 2×/day | Consistent overposting reduces per-post engagement |
| Repost other people's clips | Meta explicitly deprioritises unoriginal reposts and low-value reaction content |
| Chase a viral moment outside the niche | A viral video that brings the wrong audience makes every future video perform worse |
| Add a 6th platform | You have one page at 584 followers. Focus is the strategy. |

# PART 9 · TRACKING

## 9.1 · The instrument

`TRACKER.xlsx` (built alongside this file), four tabs:

| Tab | What it holds | Filled |
|---|---|---|
| **Post Log** | one row per post: date, day, format, pillar, hook style, A/B version, views, 3-sec rate, avg watch, saves, shares, comments, follows, convos, verdict | +24h after every post |
| **Weekly** | auto-rolled: the 8 KPIs vs target, week over week | Sunday |
| **Leads** | every DM/convo: date, source post, model, budget, timing, temperature, dealer routed, outcome | on arrival + at +7d |
| **Experiments** | one row per hook A/B and per Experiment-day format: hypothesis, result, verdict, ported to PLAYBOOK? | on resolve |

**Post Log is the one that matters.** It is the artefact that finally closes the standing
gap named in `CLAUDE.md`: *"Nothing has ever been posted. Every retention figure is
engineered-for, not measured."* That is no longer true — the page has posted — but the
numbers still live nowhere durable. A weekly HTML that gets regenerated is not a record.
**You cannot see a trend in a file that gets overwritten.**

## 9.2 · The rituals

```
DAILY · 10 min, 9am
  Log yesterday's post at +24h. Check overnight DMs. Note anything anomalous.

+24h PER POST · 5 min       (extends 15-launch-protocol PART 4)
  Pull: 3-sec rate · avg % viewed · saves · shares · comments · follows · convos
  Verdict, one line: what the FIRST THREE SECONDS did, and why.
  Route:  3-sec ≥25% → hook style goes in PLAYBOOK as proven
          3-sec <20% → REPOST WITH HOOK B at the next slot
          saves high, views low → format is right, hook is wrong
          views high, saves/convos zero → wrong audience; check the hook's promise

WEEKLY · 60 min, Sunday 4pm            (before writing next week's plan)
  1  Roll up the 8 KPIs vs target
  2  Best post and worst post — name the CAUSE, not the number
  3  Hook A/B results → PLAYBOOK.md "Patterns added"
  4  Lead pipeline: how many, what temperature, what's stuck
  5  Decide next week's Experiment
  6  THEN run WEEKLY-TASK.md

MONTHLY · 2h, first Monday
  1  30-day trend on every KPI (trend, not snapshot)
  2  Dealer reports out (§8.2)
  3  Audience check in Page Insights: is the age/gender/location mix moving
     toward Avatar A and B, or away? A growing page with the wrong audience
     is a worse position than a flat one with the right audience.
  4  Re-verify posting times against your own insights
  5  Kill/scale decisions (§9.4)
```

## 9.3 · The KPI set — 8 numbers, nothing more

| # | Metric | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **3-sec rate** ⭐ | The north star. Everything else is downstream. |
| 2 | Avg % viewed | Tells you whether the body delivers what the hook promised |
| 3 | Saves + shares | The strongest Reels ranking signals; also the 60-day-window mechanism |
| 4 | Comments | Meaningful Social Interaction; your weakest number |
| 5 | Net follows | The compounding asset |
| 6 | **WhatsApp / Messenger convos** ⭐ | The money metric |
| 7 | Outbound comments made | The only *input* metric here. Track it because it is the one you fully control. |
| 8 | Active dealer partners | The business |

**Vanity metrics to ignore:** total views, total likes, reach. They move for reasons you
can't act on.

## 9.4 · Decision rules — written in advance, so they aren't argued about later

```
A FORMAT IS SCALED    3-sec ≥25% on 3 consecutive runs → +1 slot/week
A FORMAT IS KILLED    3-sec <15% on 3 consecutive runs → dropped, slot reassigned
A HOOK STYLE IS BANKED  ≥25% twice → PLAYBOOK "Patterns added"
AN EXPERIMENT WINS    beats the 4-week median of the slot it displaced → becomes permanent
A POST IS BOOSTED     only after 48h organic AND 3-sec ≥30% AND ≥1 convo. Never a loser.
A DEALER IS DROPPED   3 months, zero enquiries routed, and he won't give document access
THE PLAN IS REWRITTEN 4 consecutive weeks with no movement on the north star
                      → the problem is the strategy, not the execution. Come back to this file.
```

## 9.5 · What to check in Meta Business Suite, and where

```
Insights → Content       per-post: 3-sec plays, avg watch time, saves, shares
Insights → Audience      age / gender / location — is it Avatar A and B?
Insights → Results       net follows (the number to watch weekly)
Inbox                    response time — keep it under 15 min in business hours
Creator Studio → Monetization → Eligibility    the only trustworthy source for thresholds
```
