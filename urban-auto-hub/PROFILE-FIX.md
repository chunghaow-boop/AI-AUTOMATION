## THE HEADLINE

I told you last session the problem was the 3-second rate. **Having now seen the profile,
the 3-second rate is a symptom, not the disease.**

The page is running **three audiences and two businesses under four brand names.** Facebook
cannot build an audience profile for a page that behaves like four pages, so its test pools
are badly targeted, so the hook fails against people who were never the right viewers.

> **You are not losing followers because the videos are bad. You are losing them because a
> person who follows for recond car advice gets served a social-media-agency ad, a
> motivational quote, and a teaser for something that never arrives.**

That is the −15.

# PART 4 · THE FIXES — PASTE-READY

## FIX 1 · Pick one name. Today. *(30 min, biggest single lever)*

**Recommendation: the public page is `Urban Auto Hub`, and every other name comes off it.**

- Urban Auto Hub is the page name, the cover, the URL and the reviews. It has the equity.
- **Talyx is your agency's name — it belongs on `talyxmarketing.com` and in dealer pitches, not on a buyer-facing car page.**
- **Nevspeed** is a third identity with no equity. Retire it or make it Nev's *personal* handle, separate from this page.

```
DO THIS

```

> One brand mark on the video, bottom-right, small: **UAH**. That is all.

## FIX 2 · Profile picture *(1 hour)*

A profile picture has one job: **be recognisable at 40 pixels.**

```
SPEC
· 1080×1080, circular safe area (Facebook crops to a circle)
· ONE element only: the letters UAH, or a single bold glyph
· HIGH contrast — light mark on dark, or dark on light. Not dark-red on dark-maroon.
· NO wordmark. "TALYX" and "URBAN AUTO HUB" are both unreadable at 40px.
· NO globe, no gradient, no thin ring.
· TEST: shrink it to 40×40 and look at it on your phone. If you can't tell what it
  is in half a second, it fails.
```

**Stronger option worth testing: use Nev's face.** Your best post ever was emotional and
human. A face at 40px reads instantly, and people follow people. Split-test it for 30 days
against the wordmark.

you have nothing to sell.**
