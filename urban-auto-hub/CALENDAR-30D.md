## THREE REVISIONS YOUR BRIEF FORCES

I got things wrong in the last two sessions. Stated plainly so the calendar makes sense.

### 1 · The niche is much bigger than I assumed — and much better

I built everything around *"we promote dealer stock."* You've now said you can **import any
car, from Japan or elsewhere, built to the buyer's exact spec.**

That is not a recond page. **That is a bespoke sourcing business**, and it is a far stronger
position:

| "Recond car page" | **"We find the exact car that doesn't exist here"** |
|---|---|
| Competes with every dealer in Malaysia | Competes with almost nobody |
| Content = reviews of cars anyone can see | Content = a machine nobody else shows |
| Trust asset = your opinion | Trust asset = auction sheets, grades, documents |
| Buyer picks from what you have | **Buyer describes what they want** |

Every Malaysian car page reviews cars. **Almost none show the sourcing machine** — the
auction sheet, the bid, the shipping, the customs, the timeline. That machine is your
content engine and your moat at the same time. This calendar is built on it.

### 2 · I told you to cut KL and Sandakan. That was half wrong.

I was right that **geography is your trust moat** — a KL page can't speak about the
Penampang stretch and mean it. I was wrong to conclude the *service* should be KK-only. A
car sourced from a Japanese auction lands wherever you ship it. Sourcing is inherently
national.

**The correct split:**

```
IDENTITY / PRESENCE   Kota Kinabalu.  This is who we are and where we stand.
SERVICE AREA          Seluruh Malaysia. This is where we deliver.
```

So the bio stays KK-anchored, but the About and the pinned post say *"kami hantar ke
seluruh Malaysia."* Sandakan and KL come off the **cover** (visual clutter, weak claim) and
go into the **About** (factual reach). Local identity, national service — that combination
is stronger than either alone.

### 3 · My avatars were wrong for a high-spending audience

I made the Family Upgrader primary and built around MPVs and monthly instalments. For
buyers with real spending power, **monthly-payment framing actively repels.** "Kalau gaji
korang RM5,000…" tells a towkay this page isn't for him. Replaced:

| | Avatar | Who | What stops them | Converts by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **The Spec Hunter** ⭐ | 35–55, business owner, knows exactly what he wants — this grade, this colour, these options. Frustrated that local stock never matches. | Auction sheets, spec talk, "local only comes in 2 colours" | **WhatsApp with a spec** |
| **B** | **Quiet Money** | 45–65, towkay or senior professional. Buys for status, hates being sold to, never comments. | Rare units, "first in Sabah", zero-hassle framing | **Silent DM** |
| **C** | **The Value Analyst** | 30–50, wealthy but rational. "Value for money" means *the maths works*, not *it's cheap*. | Cost stacks, depreciation curves, duty breakdowns | Saves first, DMs later |
| **D** | **Enthusiast with Budget** | 28–45, JDM/Euro. Wants the unicorn: FL5, GR Yaris, LC300 ZX, Alphard Exec Lounge, R34. | Rarity, spec detail, sound | Comments hardest, shares most |
| **E** | **The Dealer** | KK dealer principals — still the B2B payer | Watching whether your page moves metal | Retainer |

**Register rule that follows from this:** absolute prices, never monthly instalments, as the
lead number. Say `RM___` on screen; put *"anggaran monthly"* in the caption if needed, never
in the hook.

## ⚠ ONE COMPLIANCE QUESTION YOU MUST ANSWER BEFORE DAY 1

Importing a vehicle into Malaysia requires an **Approved Permit (AP)**. Either you hold one,
or you work with an AP-holding partner.

**Every CTA in this calendar is worded to be true in both cases** — *"WhatsApp kami dengan
spec korang"* / *"kami mula cari"* — never *"kami import untuk korang"*. Do not upgrade that
wording unless you actually hold the permit.

Also carried forward from `WEEKLY-TASK.md`, unchanged:


### The four things that would make this month a failure even if every post ships

1. Posting into the unfixed profile. **Do `PROFILE-FIX.md` 1–8 first.**
2. Skipping the 90-minute daily engagement block. Seventy-five outbound comments a week is the growth engine; the calendar is only the fuel.
