Before you shoot

  • Ask the dealer the day before which one unit he wants pushed on Merdeka day, and book him for 90 minutes on camera. He drives, he talks.
  • Get in writing: model, year, mileage, grade, price, anggaran monthly, and the exact spelling of his business name. Off his documents, not memory.
  • Confirm the unit is not sold on the morning of the shoot. Ask again before you post.
  • Agree the two features with him beforehand and make sure both are demonstrable on camera. No feature that only exists on the spec sheet.
  • Agree the one honest downside he is willing to say out loud. If he will not name one, the twist is gone and the video is just an advert — pick another dealer or another day.
  • Scout the road stretch: one rough or steep piece of Sabah road you can legally film on, 10 minutes from the showroom. Do not go hunting on the day.
  • Check insurance and that the dealer is happy to drive with a camera mounted.
