⚗ EXPERIMENTHook #5 Cold soundTwist Expectation flipCTA Next-episodePorted from food / fitness
ROLE BOUNDARY. This is the safest video of the week on the boundary, and that is part of why it was chosen. Nev is filming his own working day as an agency person — driving to dealers and asking questions. He never opens a bonnet, never inspects, never quotes a price himself. Every car shown belongs to a named dealer.

What this video has to do

Test whether process content works on this page. The format is lifted wholesale from the food/fitness “what I eat in a day” timestamp genre — nothing in Malaysian recond content is doing it. The payoff is that people see the work of asking, which is the only thing Talyx actually sells.

Before you shoot

  • Pick a day you are genuinely visiting two or more dealers. Do not stage it — staged process content reads false immediately.
  • Tell each dealer beforehand that you will be filming a short segment at his lot, and get a yes.
  • You need one honest verdict at the end — “satu je saya rasa berbaloi”. Decide that after the day, not before.
  • Shoot far more than you need. This format is built in the edit, not on the day.
  • Keep a note of real times as you go. The timestamps must be true — if you arrive at 9:40, the card says 9:40.

Gear & setup

  • Osmo Pocket 3, chest mount or dash mount for the driving, handheld for the lots
  • Mic on Nev all day — you will not know in advance which line matters
  • Charge everything the night before. This is a nine-hour shoot on one battery budget.
  • Shoot the 7am start even if you feel silly. The cold engine sound is the hook and it cannot be recreated at noon.

Lines that must land exactly

  • "Kami bantu dealer promote kereta ni." — once, SHOT 5.
  • Every dealer visited is named on screen at their segment.
  • Timestamps must be the real times. Do not invent a schedule.
  • Nev does not quote any price in this video. If a price appears, it is on a card, credited to the dealer.
  • The follow-up promised in SHOT 7 must be posted Friday or Saturday.

Everything else marked SAY IT YOUR WAY is intent, not wording. Nev should paraphrase in his own mouth — reading it verbatim is what makes it sound stiff.
