ROLE BOUNDARY. Comparing two ownership routes is commentary and is fine. What is not fine: implying we can get you either one, or that we have priced them. Both figures are attributed — the recond price to the dealer partner, the new price to the official list price.

What this video has to do

Drive comments. This is the single most important KPI on the page (4 in 28 days, target 40) and comparison is the proven mechanic. The whole video is engineered so that Nev never gives the answer — because the moment he does, the comment disappears.

Before you shoot

  • Pick a model where the dealer partner actually has the recond unit in stock. The comparison must be real and shoppable.
  • Get the official new-car price from the manufacturer's own site, screenshot it, and use that figure. Do not estimate.
  • Decide your flip in advance: the one Sabah-specific factor where the obvious winner loses. Service centre distance, parts wait time, or resale in KK — pick one, not three.
  • Verify the flip. If you cannot point at something real, drop the flip and pick another factor.
  • Do not shoot this as an argument for recond. If it reads as an advert for our own inventory, it will not generate debate.

Gear & setup

  • Osmo Pocket 3 for the car footage; the split screen is built in the edit
  • Shoot both sides in similar light or the comparison looks rigged
  • Mic on Nev
  • You need the manufacturer price screenshot as an on-screen source card
