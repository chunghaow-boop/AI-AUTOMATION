Sabtu · 29 Ogos // Showroom day dengan dealer partner

Facebook Live 15–20 min · fallback BTS Reel 60s · shot at the partner showroom
Hook #8 Mid-action startCTA ConnectStyle Behind the scenesLive 15–20 min
ROLE BOUNDARY. The dealer hosts. Nev asks. The dealer walks his own row, opens his own cars, quotes his own prices. Nev never quotes a price himself — when a viewer asks one, he turns to the dealer and asks him to say it out loud. Nev never opens a bonnet, never assesses condition, never says “kami dah check”.

What this video has to do

Two things money cannot buy. One: a Live pushes a notification to all 584 followers — the cheapest reach on the page. Two: event-style posts are historically the only thing that has produced real comments here. The Live is also a footage farm — two or three clips from it become next week's reels.

Before you shoot

  • Confirm the Live slot with the dealer at least two days ahead, and confirm he is comfortable being live and unedited.
  • Agree in advance which units are fair game and which are not. Nothing sold, nothing under negotiation.
  • Agree the boundary out loud with him: he says the prices, he answers condition questions. Tell him Nev will keep turning to him — that is by design, not distrust.
  • Post a reminder to the page 2 hours before, and again 15 minutes before.
  • Have someone watching comments on a second phone so Nev can be fed questions. Doing both alone is why most dealer Lives die.
  • Check the showroom wifi. Test the stream for 60 seconds before going live for real.

Gear & setup

  • Phone on a gimbal for the Live — Osmo Pocket 3 as the B-camera capturing clean footage for next week's reels
  • Record locally as well as streaming. Facebook's Live download is lower quality and you will want the clean file.
  • Wireless mic on the dealer, second on Nev. A showroom is loud and echoey.
  • Power bank. Twenty minutes of Live plus B-cam will drain a phone fast.
  • A written list of 5 fallback questions for when the comments go quiet — they will, around minute 8

Lines that must land exactly

  • "Kami bantu dealer promote kereta ni." — once, in the first two minutes.
  • Nev never states a price. Every price question goes to the dealer, out loud, on stream.
  • The dealer is thanked by name at the close.
  • No unit that is sold or under negotiation appears.
  • No comparison to any other dealer, shop or salesman — not even a joke.

Everything else marked SAY IT YOUR WAY is intent, not wording. Nev should paraphrase in his own mouth — reading it verbatim is what makes it sound stiff.

Export

Stream at the highest quality the showroom wifi holds. Keep the local recording — the Facebook download is worse and you need the clean file. Within 24 hours, pull 2–3 clips for next week's reels and note the timecodes in PLAYBOOK.md.
