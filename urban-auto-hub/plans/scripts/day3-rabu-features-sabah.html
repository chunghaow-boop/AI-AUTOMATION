Rabu · 26 Ogos // Dua benda ni — jalan Sabah baru korang faham

Reel · 60s · vertical 9:16 · shot on a real road, not in the showroom
Hook #3 Bold claimTwist Good-news-then-flawCTA QuestionStyle Industry valueRuntime 60s
ROLE BOUNDARY. Nev is commenting on how the car behaves on roads he drives — that is allowed and it is his real experience. He is not grading the car, not certifying it, not pricing it. The flaw he names must be one the dealer has already disclosed, quoted as the dealer’s disclosure — never Nev’s own finding.

What this video has to do

Repeat the recipe that produced the fastest video this page has ever had (LX600, 526 views in 13 hours): two real features, tied to real Sabah roads, no showroom talk. The flaw at 42s is what stops it being an advert.

Before you shoot

  • Ask the dealer which two features he thinks matter most on Sabah roads — then verify them yourself before filming.
  • Ask him directly: “Ada apa-apa yang saya patut sebut yang tak sempurna?” Whatever he says is your 42s beat. Write it down in his words.
  • Get written permission to take the unit out on the road, and confirm insurance covers it.
  • Scout the road first. You need one stretch with a real reason — broken tarmac, a steep kampung climb, or heavy rain if you can get it.
  • Price and anggaran monthly in writing, plus the dealer's business name spelling.

Gear & setup

  • Osmo Pocket 3 — window mount or chest mount for the driving beats
  • Wireless mic on Nev; road noise will eat the built-in mic
  • Shoot the road beats first while the light holds; static talking beats can be picked up after
  • If it rains, drop everything and shoot — rain footage on a Sabah road is worth more than a perfect blue sky

Lines that must land exactly

  • "Kami bantu dealer promote kereta ni." — once, SHOT 2.
  • The flaw must be attributed to the dealer’s own disclosure on camera. Never “I found”, never “they didn’t tell me”.
  • anggaran said aloud and on screen with the monthly figure.
  • Dealer partner: [name] on screen and in caption.

Everything else marked SAY IT YOUR WAY is intent, not wording. Nev should paraphrase in his own mouth — reading it verbatim is what makes it sound stiff.
