What this video has to do

Two jobs. One: give the page a live, dated shop window in its highest-attention slot. Two: harvest comments cheaply — 'comment nombor' is the lowest-effort comment ask we have, and comments are the weakest number on the dashboard.

Before you shoot

  • Get the final list of units from the dealer on Monday night. Anything that sells before Tuesday morning comes off the carousel — never post a sold unit.
  • For each unit collect, in writing: model, year, mileage, grade, price, anggaran monthly. All read off his documents, not from memory.
  • Ask which units he actually wants pushed. Do not guess.
  • Shoot all 6 units in one session at the same time of day — mixed lighting across cards makes the carousel look scraped together.
  • Do not write 'last unit' unless it is literally the last unit.

Gear & setup

  • Osmo Pocket 3 or phone — stills, not video. Highest resolution available.
  • Shoot at golden hour or full shade. Midday sun on a windscreen kills the shot.
  • Same angle for every car: front 3/4, camera at headlight height, car filling ~70% of frame. Consistency is what makes the set look professional.
  • Wipe the plate area — you will be covering or blurring it anyway, but a dirty plate reads as a dirty car.
  • Bring the price list on paper so you can label files as you shoot.

Lines that must land exactly

  • Caption must read “dari dealer partner kami” — never “stock kami”, never “kereta kami”.
  • Dealer partner: [name] appears on every card and in the caption.
  • Every price card carries the word anggaran on the monthly figure.
  • No unit that is already sold. No “last unit!” unless literally true.

Everything else marked SAY IT YOUR WAY is intent, not wording. Nev should paraphrase in his own mouth — reading it verbatim is what makes it sound stiff.

Export

Carousel, 4:5 or 1:1, consistent across all six. Pin it Tuesday morning — unpin last week’s first. Caption ends with the question and the WhatsApp number. Reply to every comment within the hour; a 'comment nombor' post that goes unanswered kills the mechanic for next week.
