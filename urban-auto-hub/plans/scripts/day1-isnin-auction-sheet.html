Isnin · 24 Ogos // Auction sheet — saya minta dealer baca

Reel · 70s · vertical 9:16 · burned-in BM captions · shot at the dealer's showroom
Hook #4 Everything on the tableTwist Music cut to silenceCTA SaveStyle TransparencyRuntime 70s
ROLE BOUNDARY. Nev does not touch the auction sheet at any point in this video. The dealer holds it, the dealer points at it, the dealer explains it. Nev's only job is to ask the question a buyer would be too shy to ask. If Nev ends up holding the document, the take is unusable — reshoot it.

What this video has to do

Make one buyer feel like they finally understand what an auction sheet says — and understand it from the dealer's mouth, with Nev sitting where the viewer would sit. Success is a save and a WhatsApp, not a like.

Before you shoot

  • Ask the dealer the day before: is he willing to be filmed reading the auction sheet, and can we show the sheet on camera? Get a yes on both. No yes, no shoot.
  • Ask him to pick one unit where the sheet is honest and readable — including any repair marks. A clean-looking sheet with a repair on it is better content than a perfect one.
  • Confirm the mileage on the sheet matches the meter before you roll. If they don't match, stop and pick a different unit — do not film it and 'explain' the gap.
  • Get the price and the anggaran monthly in writing from the dealer, plus the exact spelling of his business name for the credit.
  • Check the sheet for the previous owner's name or any personal data — cover it with a card or shoot around it.
  • Table by a window, sheet flat, no glare. Test one frame before you call the dealer over.

Gear & setup

  • Osmo Pocket 3, 9:16, 4K/30 or 1080/60 if you want the meter slow-mo
  • Wireless mic on the dealer — he is doing most of the talking in this one. Second mic on Nev if you have it.
  • Small tripod or gorillapod for the locked-off table shots (SHOT 1 and 6)
  • A plain card to cover any personal data on the sheet
  • Bring a cloth — fingerprints on the meter glass read badly in CU

SHOT 10:00 – 0:03The document lands
Framing
ECU, top-down. The sheet fills the frame edge to edge. Dealer's hands only — no faces, no car.
Camera move
Locked off. Osmo on the tripod at the table edge, pointing straight down. Zero movement. Do not let the gimbal drift or re-level mid-shot.
Action
The dealer slides the auction sheet flat into frame and squares it up with both hands, then lifts his hands away. One smooth slide — not a drop, not a slap.
Expression
No faces in this shot. The hands carry it: unhurried, careful, like the document matters.
Audio
SILENCE. No music at all. Room tone only — you want the paper sound and nothing else.
On-screen text
AUCTION SHEET — small, top-left, white, no box, no animation.
Edit note
Hard in on the first frame of the slide. No fade up. The first thing the viewer sees is already the document.

Lines that must land exactly

  • "Kami bantu dealer promote kereta ni." — once, in SHOT 2. Never twice, never expanded, nothing added about licences or why we don’t sell.
  • "Harga dia RM___. Anggaran bulanan RM___." — the word anggaran must be said out loud and appear on screen.
  • "Unit ni dari dealer partner kami, [name]." — the dealer is credited by name on camera and in the caption.
  • "Tanya dulu, baru beli." — proposed sign-off, same every video. Confirm before the first shoot.

Everything else marked SAY IT YOUR WAY is intent, not wording. Nev should paraphrase in his own mouth — reading it verbatim is what makes it sound stiff.
