ROLE BOUNDARY. Nev does not touch the auction sheet at any point. The dealer holds it, points at it, explains it. If Nev ends up holding the document, the take is unusable — reshoot.
What this video has to do. Make one buyer finally understand what an auction sheet says — from the dealer's mouth, with Nev sitting where the viewer would sit. Success is a save and a WhatsApp, not a like.

Before you shoot

  • Ask the dealer the day before: will he be filmed reading the sheet, and can we show it? Yes to both or no shoot.
  • Ask him to pick one unit where the sheet is honest and readable — including repair marks. A sheet with a repair on it is better content than a perfect one.
  • Confirm mileage on the sheet matches the meter before rolling. If they don't match, pick another unit — do not film it and explain the gap.
  • Price + anggaran monthly in writing. Exact spelling of his business name.
  • Cover any previous-owner name or personal data on the sheet.
  • Table by a window, no glare. Test one frame before calling the dealer over.

Gear & setup

  • Osmo Pocket 3, 9:16, 4K/30
  • Wireless mic on the dealer — he talks most in this one
  • Small tripod for the locked-off table shots
  • A card to cover personal data
  • Cloth for the meter glass — fingerprints read badly in CU
