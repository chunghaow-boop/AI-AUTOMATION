## THE THREE MIX RULES (from the Sound Engineer, file 19)
1. **One bed per video.** Never two tracks. One continuous bed is what makes 9 clips feel like one film.
2. **Bed sits at −18 to −20 LUFS** so foley and voice live above it. Master the whole video to −14.
3. **Duck the bed to 10–20% for ~1s before the twist.** Every format. The reveal lands in near-silence.
