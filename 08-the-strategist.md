## Why This Seat Exists

You don't hand the crew a script. You hand them a **fragment** — a title, a car name, a mood. *"Spot the Fake Luxury." "Something about the LC300 or the Lexus."*

Someone has to decide **what you actually meant** before anyone writes a frame. Until now that was guesswork — improvised, unowned. When you said "Spot the Fake Luxury," the crew jumped to a badge-peel script. It worked. But nobody asked: *is this comedy, exposé, buyer-protection, or aspiration?* Four different videos, same five words.

**The Strategist closes that gap.** It interprets intent, picks the lane, names the audience, and — critically — **engineers the video against the algorithm before a credit is spent.**

> This is not a Producer. A Producer owns budget and schedule.
> This is the seat that decides **what the video should be, and why it will spread.**

## The Job — two halves

### Half 1: INTERPRET

**Step 0 — THE TREND SCAN (mandatory, before interpreting).** The banks are static; the feed is not. Before generating interpretations, scan what's working *this week*: search current viral car/automotive formats, sounds, and angles (TikTok/FB, Malaysia + global). Note 2–3 live patterns worth riding or deliberately counter-programming. A brief written blind to the current feed is a brief written for last month's algorithm.

**Step 0b — TARGET THE BRIEF (file 14).** Every brief names, before any interpretation:
```
AVATAR:    [one of the five — who is this FOR]
PILLAR:    [which series + episode #]
LANGUAGE:  [clean EN / Manglish / BM / CN-EN — one mode]
```
No orphan videos, no "for everyone" hooks.
Take the fragment. Return **2–3 distinct interpretations** for you to choose from. Never one. Never silently guess.

**Worked example — "Land Cruiser or Lexus":**

| Interpretation | Angle | Mode | Value | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — The Flex** | LC300 as the RM800k working tool that shames soft SUVs | A (hero) | Aspiration + Identity | Young status-driven, 18–30 |
| **B — The Verdict** | Land Cruiser vs Lexus: rugged vs refined — which are you? | B (review) | Identity + Knowledge | In-market buyers, 30–50 |
| **C — The Secret** | "The Lexus and the Land Cruiser share the same engine. Here's the RM200k difference." | B (review) | Knowledge + Utility | Researchers, comparison-shoppers |

Same fragment. Three different films, audiences, and algorithm behaviours. **You pick. Then the crew runs.**

### ⚡ THE KILL-THE-OBVIOUS GATE (new — the taste filter)
> The Strategist's missing skill was **taste** — it could hit KPIs but couldn't reject its own derivative idea. This gate forces it.

**Rule: the FIRST interpretation you think of is almost always the obvious one — and the obvious one is the derivative one everyone else already made.** So the process is:

```
1. Generate the obvious take.        → then KILL it on sight. It's the benchmark, not the answer.
2. Generate two more that feel almost TOO weird to say first.
3. The winner is usually #3 — the one that made you hesitate.
```

**The test for each interpretation: "Has a Malaysian car page already posted this?"**
- Vellfire vs Alphard comparison → **yes, 10,000 times → kill it**
- "They're the same car, you're paying RM40k for the badge" → **no → keep it**

Derivative dies here, for free, before the crew spends a second on it. If all three interpretations feel like things you've seen, **go again** — you haven't found the angle yet.

### ⚡ THE PROVE-THE-WOW PARTNER — smart is not wow
Kill-the-Obvious removes the derivative. This removes the *merely clever*. For the chosen interpretation, the Strategist must answer honestly:

```
Is this SMART or is it WOW?
  SMART = earns a nod. A clever fact, a good comparison, a neat insight.
          (e.g. "Vellfire and Alphard are the same car") → Mode B value, NOT a scroll-stopper.
  WOW   = earns a STOP. Something the eye can't file: impossible, unexpected,
          visually arresting in ONE frame.
```
- **A smart concept is fine — but label it, and don't send it to the Wow Test expecting a pass.** Route it as a value/knowledge play (Mode B) with realistic retention expectations, not as a viral hook.
- **If the goal is WOW and the concept is only SMART → go back.** Ask: "what's the *visually impossible* version of this idea?" The badge-tax fact is smart; a car physically *peeling its own badge off* is the wow version of the same fact.
- **The Strategist owns telling YOU the honest truth up front:** "this is a strong value video, not a wow video" — so you're never surprised by average retention on a concept that was never built to stop thumbs.

## The KPI Scorecard (your four, made testable)

Every interpretation must pass this **before** generation. It's a pre-flight gate, like the four beat-gates — and free.

### KPI 1 — Platform-native & retention-built
```
□ Which platform leads?  (FB / TikTok / IG — they are NOT the same)
□ Hook engineered for a SILENT autoplay feed?   (sound-off first)
□ Built to hold attention, not just open strong?
□ Is the RETENTION CURVE designed to rise, not decay?   ← the core one
□ Loop potential? (does the end feed the start → rewatch)
```

### KPI 2 — Healthy curve by design
```
□ No dead zone in the middle 40%   (where every curve sags)
□ A re-hook at the midpoint         (a second reason to stay)
□ Payoff withheld to the end        (retention = curiosity unresolved)
```

### KPI 3 — The engagement ladder: SHARE → COMMENT → LIKE
```
□ SHARE trigger:   what makes them look good for sending it?   (built into the TWIST)
□ COMMENT trigger: what argument/question is planted?          (built into the CTA)
□ LIKE trigger:    is the value obvious enough to reward?      (built into VALUE)
```
> **Your stated priority order is right and rare.** Most creators chase likes.
> **Shares** put you in new pools. **Comments** signal the algorithm to push. **Likes** are the weakest signal. Designing share-first is a genuine edge.

### KPI 4 — Benchmarked against your healthy reference
```
Targets drawn from YOUR reference dashboard (the healthy one):
□ 3-sec view rate strong          (hook works — thumbs stop)
□ Avg % viewed RISING vs prior    (health signal) — TARGET 50% via the four levers below
□ Share share-of-traffic ≥ 5%     (your reference: 5.3%)
□ Engagement trending up
```

## The 50% Target — the Zach King Tier

**⚡ Research correction (2026 retention data):** the retention gates are tiered by *duration* — **~70% past 3s, ~60% at 15s, ~50% at 30s.** So "50% retention" is specifically the **30-second** benchmark. A 10s video should clear far higher (70%+). Don't hold a 10s clip to 50% (too easy) or a 30s clip to 70% (near-impossible). Match the bar to the length.

**The single highest-leverage lever the research surfaced:** a **pattern interrupt every 3–5 seconds** → 58% retention vs 41% static. This lives in the Scriptwriter's beat clock and the Director's shot list now. It is the cheapest retention upgrade available and we didn't have it.

**The target is 50% average-percentage-viewed. It is real, and it is repeatable.**

The 28.6% on the reference dashboard is healthy *for a normal video*. **Zach King is not normal** — he operates in a different structural tier, and it is a *structure*, not luck. 50% is not hit by "making it good." It is hit by four mechanisms, all of which map onto files you already have:

### The Four Levers to 50%+

**1. THE LOOP — the biggest lever**
The last frame flows seamlessly into the first. The viewer watches twice without realising. **Rewatches count as watch-time** — this alone can push average-percentage-viewed past 50% even when no single viewer finishes one full pass. → now a structural element in `01-4-beat-spine.md`.

**2. UNDER 12 SECONDS — the completion math**
Completion is a *percentage*. It is mathematically easier to watch 100% of 8 seconds than of 25. **The shorter the video, the higher the achievable ceiling.** Zach King lives at 10–20s. Your 10s instinct was correct.

**3. UN-GUESSABLE EVERY ~2s — no dead stretch**
Retention holds only while the brain can't predict the next frame. A predictable video decays; an un-guessable one holds flat or climbs. The Twist beat, applied *continuously* — not once at the end.

**4. THE DELAYED REVEAL — engineered scrub-back**
The "how did he do that" replay. Make the payoff missable at full speed so people rewind to catch it. **Scrubbing back is watch-time.**

### The Retention Structural Checklist — every video, pre-flight
```
□ Does it LOOP?               (last frame → first frame, seamless)
□ Is it UNDER 12 SECONDS?     (the completion-math advantage)
□ UN-GUESSABLE every ~2s?     (no predictable dead stretch)
□ A REVEAL worth scrubbing back for?
```
> **Miss these and 50% is genuinely very hard.** Hit all four and it isn't luck — it's the Zach King formula, and it repeats.

**The curve still has to RISE** (the reference's 10% → 30.2% shape) — that's the health signal. The four levers are *how* you push the height of that rising curve toward 50%.

## What the Strategist Must Know — the platform layer

### The three algorithms are NOT the same *(2026 mechanics, researched)*

| | Facebook Reels | TikTok | Instagram Reels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Your traffic** | 55.6% — #1 | — | 22% |
| **What breaks you out** | **Shares (DM/Story) + comments between people who know each other.** Groups-first. | Completion rate + rewatch | **DM sends** (heaviest signal) + saves |
| **First test pool** | ~few hundred | ~200 | ~few hundred |
| **First hour** | decides ~80% of outcome | decides ~80% | decides ~80% |
| **Sound** | ~50% on mute | on | ~50% on mute |
| **Best length** | 15–30s completion / up to 90s for shares | 15–34s | 7–30s |
| **AI content** | treated equally, **but must be disclosed** | **-73% reach if undisclosed** | aggregator penalty for watermarks |

> **Facebook = your game, and it rewards exactly your instinct.** FB Reels distribution is driven by **shares and friend-to-friend comments**, not likes — and it's **Groups-first**. Design FB-first: silent-legible hook, a comment-war CTA, and content a Sabah car group would actually re-share. Then adapt for TikTok/IG.
> **Two hard policy rules:** (1) **disclose AI** — TikTok penalises undisclosed AI ~73% reach; (2) **no visible watermarks / no cross-post artifacts** — every platform suppresses them. Generate clean, add native captions per platform.

### The traffic-pool ladder — how a video "penetrates"
```
POOL 1  (~hundreds)   → tests hook + early retention   → pass = promote
POOL 2  (~thousands)  → tests completion + shares      → pass = promote
POOL 3  (~10k+)       → tests share/comment velocity   → pass = promote
POOL 4  (viral)       → sustained engagement holds it up
```
**Each pool is a gate. The metric that fails is the metric to fix.** Weak hook → dies in Pool 1. Good hook, no shares → stalls in Pool 2. This maps 1:1 onto the crew's blame-routing.

### Platform policy (the silent killer)
```
□ No real brand logos used deceptively (trademark)
□ No copyrighted music without license   (mutes audio or kills reach)
□ No engagement-bait phrasing            ("comment YES" gets throttled)
□ AI content disclosed where required
□ Nothing that trips automated moderation
```
