Pipeline Health Check

I tested every part of your video factory today. Here is what I found — in plain words.

Think of it like a factory with 5 security guards

Every video passes through the same line. Each guard can stop a bad video from moving forward. The last guard stops anything from reaching you.

1
The Plan

Before anything is made, everything is decided on paper: the hook, the twist, the CTA, the music, the captions. No plan = the video cannot start. (This is what saved us — the old bad videos had no plan at all.)

↓
2
Guard 1 — Plan Checker (planqc)

Reads the plan and asks 49 questions. "Where is the hook? Where is the CTA? Did you learn from past mistakes?" If any answer is missing, it refuses to let the video be made.

↓
3
The Storyboard + Board Checker

Makes the picture-plan you review, and checks the board itself is honest before showing you. Today it correctly refused a board that showed the wrong image.

↓
4
The Edit

The video is cut exactly as the plan says. The builder must also write down where every cut is — this is its "receipt" so the guards can check its work later.

↓
5
Guard 2 — Video Checker (verify)

Watches the finished video and measures ~21 things: loudness, black frames, repeated shots, captions in the right place. This guard works, but some of its rules are old — see below.

↓
6
Guard 3 — The Final Boss (predeliver)

The last gate before you. It checks three things: (1) did all the work actually happen, (2) did all the other guards pass it, (3) for every warning, did I actually LOOK at the frames — I am not allowed to talk my way past a warning anymore. Only when this says CLEARED do you see the video.

↓
7
YOU

Your eye is the final-final check. Anything you catch becomes a written lesson (we have 183 now) and the next plan must read those lessons before it can pass Guard 1.

The good news

WORKING

The factory line is in the right order and every guard showed up for work today. I tested each one with a deliberately broken video — every guard caught it. The system that let 5 bad versions reach you cannot happen again, because Guard 3 now refuses any video that skipped the planning step.

The 3 problems I found

PROBLEM 1 — a sleeping guard

The caption-colour checker has never worked a single day.

Remember when you complained the caption colour clashed with the background? I built a tool to catch that... and then never connected it to the line. It has been sitting in a drawer since. It also crashes when you switch it on. Every video since then shipped with nobody checking caption contrast.

Your decision: connect it properly, or throw it away? I recommend connecting it — the complaint it answers was yours.
PROBLEM 2 — old rules judging a new kind of video

Guard 2 still judges your REAL footage with rules made for AI footage.

Examples from today: it complained your talking take doesn't cut on the music beat (correct — it cuts on your sentences, on purpose). It counted the fade-to-black transition as a "broken black frame" (it was planned). It flagged you appearing 4 times as "duplicate shots" (that is just... a person talking). None of these rules should be deleted — they just need to be taught the difference between AI footage and your camera footage. The Plan Checker already learned this; the Video Checker hasn't yet.

PROBLEM 3 — the builder forgot its receipts

The LOT edit didn't write down where its cuts were, so 9 of Guard 2's checks were blind — and nothing warned us.

I reconstructed the receipts today and 6 checks immediately came back to life. Rule going forward: an edit that doesn't write its receipts is not finished. (Also fixed today: one of the self-tests had quietly stopped proving anything — it works again and can't break the same way twice.)
